# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment and personal attacks aren't welcome here.

To report a problem, use **Report a vulnerability** on the repository's **Security** tab and say that it's about conduct. That conversation is private. Maintainers may remove comments or block people who break these rules.
